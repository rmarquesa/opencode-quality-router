# Task: decision comparison with frozen catalog evidence

Status: Draft, agent-reviewed under the user's explicit implementation and test request. This is a bounded diagnostic, not a benchmark proving the best model for development tasks.

Compare primary-model decisions with the old alphabetical default details and old policy versus the new task shortlist, recent access signal and comparison policy. Use the same frozen local catalog, quota observations, capability records and actual-task requirements in both conditions. Recent successful access may be reconstructed from native message metadata; it must not imply verified quality or quota.

Use GPT-6.1 Sol with high reasoning, six cases in fresh native OpenCode sessions per condition: two simple source/HTML tasks, two context/vision constrained frontend tasks, and two risky devops/debugging tasks. No tools, repository edits, specialist calls or metered candidate generation. Return only a structured choice, concrete per-model fit reasons, and suitable fallback list. At most 12 paired requests plus two targeted after-only checks after signal changes, 4096 output tokens per request, 60 seconds per request. The principal consumes its existing included subscription capacity; its per-model quota impact is unknown. Record native usage and provider errors privately. No automatic retry of invalid trials.

Independent checks: parse valid JSON, chosen and fallback models exist and are quota-eligible, meet known declared capabilities/context requirements, have been inspected, appear as suitable in the comparison, and include at least one distinct inspected alternative when possible. Repeated choice of the same model is accepted. Counts of different models are not a success metric. Reject quota/error/truncated answers as invalid rather than low-quality decisions. Read the decision rationales to identify unsupported claims; mechanical passes do not establish the truth of a rationale or generated-code quality.

The baseline is the source of version 0.2.1. This diagnostic supplies frozen comparison context in one request and does not recreate the entire interactive delegation loop. Native simulated-provider integration tests separately exercise consultation IDs, concurrent task isolation, comparison rejection/correction, fallback, and permission boundaries. Active user sessions must not be resumed or terminated. Reports and transcripts remain private; evaluation sessions are deleted after saving evidence.

Follow-up: repeat source-extraction and large-checkout after including dated historical access failures and freshly queried quota. Treat this as final-version validation, not additional paired evidence. Separate candidate-access probes require a positive known-working control; a shared authentication failure invalidates the probe environment and must not update production model blocks or quality grades.
